AVVA

One machine turns music into color, the other color into music

Views

Loop /?view=loop Closed loop — camera to synth to analyzer to visuals. Broadcasts its synth output to listener tabs. Video → Audio /?view=va Camera drives the synth and broadcasts, with no local renderer. The listener window draws the visuals. Audio → Video /?view=av Listener. Receives a stream from any broadcasting tab and runs the audio analyzer and GL renderer. Controller /controller/ Every tunable parameter, synced live to the other tabs in this browser. Choose the media folder here.
